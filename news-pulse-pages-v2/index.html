Today's Lead

Stablecoin Oversight Bill Advances as Senate Banking Committee Schedules Markup

The U.S. Senate Banking Committee has scheduled a markup session for the Stablecoin Transparency and Accountability Act. The bill would subject dollar-pegged stablecoins to federal reserve requirements and mandate monthly attestations from issuers — a significant escalation of oversight that could reshape the $175B+ stablecoin market. Industry groups are pushing for state-level opt-outs; Treasury is opposing carve-outs for non-bank issuers.

Why it matters: Federal stablecoin regulation would be the first direct U.S. legislative framework for digital dollar instruments. Settlement rails, issuance limits, and reserve composition requirements will all be defined — directly affecting fintech, banking, and cross-border payment infrastructure.
